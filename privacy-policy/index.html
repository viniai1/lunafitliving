Privacy Policy

A clear overview of how information is handled on Luna Fit Living.

Last updated: August 26, 2026

Information you choose to share

Luna Fit Living does not currently offer user accounts, a working newsletter signup or a contact form. You may voluntarily provide information when you contact us directly by email. We use that information only to read and respond to your message, manage the related conversation and handle any question or request you send.

Technical information and hosting

This site is hosted through GitHub Pages. When you visit, GitHub and the infrastructure used to deliver the site may process basic technical information such as an IP address, browser type, requested page and request time for security, reliability and service operation. Luna Fit Living does not currently use this information to create advertising profiles.

Analytics and consent

Luna Fit Living uses Google Analytics 4 only when you choose “Accept analytics” in the analytics notice. GA4 helps us understand general website usage, traffic sources and engagement so we can improve the site.

Analytics is optional. If you choose “Decline,” the Google Analytics tag is not loaded. Your choice is stored in your browser's local storage as either accepted or declined. This preference does not contain personal information.

If you accept analytics, Google may process technical and usage information and may use cookies or similar technologies according to Google's own privacy practices. We do not claim that analytics information is completely anonymous. Luna Fit Living does not currently use advertising pixels or analytics for personalized advertising.

Third-party links

The site may link to external websites and social platforms, including Pinterest and Instagram. Those services operate under their own privacy policies and practices. We are not responsible for how third-party websites collect, use or protect information.

Data security

We take reasonable care with information voluntarily sent to us, but no email transmission, online service or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children's privacy

Luna Fit Living is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Policy updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the site grows or its tools and services change. The date at the top of this page will show the most recent revision.

Privacy questions

For questions about this policy or information you have voluntarily provided, email hello@lunafitliving.com.
