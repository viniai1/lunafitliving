Editorial transparency

Affiliate Disclosure

Clear recommendations matter, and so does being transparent about how the site may earn revenue in the future.

Our current approach

Luna Fit Living does not currently use active affiliate links. As the site grows, some future guides or recommendations may occasionally include them.

How affiliate links may work

If you choose to make a purchase through an affiliate link, Luna Fit Living may earn a commission from the retailer or service. This would not add an additional cost to you. When affiliate links appear, the relevant content will include a clear disclosure.

Editorial independence

Recommendations should remain based on usefulness, relevance and fit for the reader. A potential commission does not guarantee that a product will be featured or positively reviewed.

Sponsored or compensated content

Any sponsored content, gifted product or other compensated relationship should be identified clearly when applicable so readers can understand the nature of the relationship.

Questions

For questions about this disclosure, email hello@lunafitliving.com.
